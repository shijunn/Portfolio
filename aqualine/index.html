How the risk score works

Each pipe gets a 0–100 risk score every scan, combining live sensor readings with the pipe's own equipment profile:

  • Vibration from traffic overhead — up to 30 pts
  • Moisture above 65%, especially during heavy rain — up to 30 pts
  • Temperature away from 28°C in the surrounding soil — up to 15 pts
  • Pressure drop below 4.2 bar, a strong sign of an active leak — up to 25 pts

Older pipes and higher-risk materials (like cast iron) tend to run higher vibration and lower pressure readings, which feeds into the same four factors.
