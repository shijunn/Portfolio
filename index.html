Underground pipe leakage predictor

A webpage that simulates and predicts leaks in underground pipes. A map shows pipes coloured by risk, with live and manual scan modes, filters by zone and condition, and a what-if simulation for a selected pipe. I made it for my Artificial Intelligence in Engineering module, using role-based, imperative and iterative prompts with Claude.

Tools: Claude, prompt design, HTML Try it liveView on GitHub

Wireless button for foam dart games

Made during my internship at 3D Print Singapore. I worked with my supervisor, a foam dart hobbyist, to decide the features, compared wireless options on cost, usability and range, and improved the design from his feedback. I also designed the PCB for the game boards. The host code and the team code are both on GitHub.

Tools: ESP32, C++, wireless protocols, PCB design Host codeTeam code

TinyBoy, a handheld arcade device

A small handheld arcade device that I designed and named TinyBoy V1 in December 2024. An ATtiny85 chip runs it, with an OLED screen, three buttons, a buzzer and a CR2032 coin cell with an on/off switch. I taught myself PCB design from online tutorials to make it.

Tools: ATtiny85, PCB design

Equipment efficiency analysis

I analysed data from Temasek Polytechnic’s Advanced Manufacturing Centre with KNIME and Tableau, then recommended actions to improve Overall Equipment Effectiveness. The plot shows a strong link between how much of a machine’s capacity is used and its OEE.

Tools: KNIME, Tableau

Online hawker food ordering system

An Object-Oriented Programming module project in Java, built in Eclipse, where customers order food and staff manage orders. I worked with a teammate: I built the customer side and she built the staff side.

When we were done, we joined both sides together and troubleshot the features that worked on our own side but stopped working after integration. We also gave each other feedback on our features, so the whole product improved and not just one side.

Tools: Java, Eclipse View on GitHub
